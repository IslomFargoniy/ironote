Ironote — Delete your account and data

Ironote stores your account and all records only on your own device. To delete them:

  1. Open the Ironote app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom menu).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, all debts, repayments and attached photos from the device. Nothing is kept on any server because the app has no server. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.
