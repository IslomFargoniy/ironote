Ironote — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Ironote ("the app") is a personal debt notebook. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: all your data stays on your device. We do not run a server, we do not collect analytics, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

1. Data you enter

You may create a local profile (name, username, phone, email, date of birth, password, avatar) and record debts (debtor name, amounts, dates, phone numbers, address, notes and photos of receipts). This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us.

2. Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate) — used only when you tap the GPS button while filling a debtor's address, to insert the current coordinates. Location is not collected in the background and is not sent anywhere.
  • Camera / Photos — used only when you choose to attach a photo to a debt or your profile, through the system camera or photo picker.
  • Biometrics — if you enable it, your device's fingerprint/face unlock is used to open the app. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessible to the app.
  • Internet — used to download official currency exchange rates from the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (cbu.uz) and to load sample images. No personal data is sent in these requests.

3. Sharing

The app does not share your data with third parties. When you tap "Call", "SMS" or "Telegram reminder", the app opens the corresponding app on your phone with a pre-filled message; sending is done by you.

4. Backups and export

You can export your data as CSV or JSON files and save them wherever you choose. Those files are under your control.

5. Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete account in the app to permanently remove your profile, debts and repayments from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all its data. See Delete account instructions.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
